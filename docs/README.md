# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A central starting point for OctoAcme's project management processes and guidance.

## Project management processes at a glance

OctoAcme manages cross-functional work through an iterative lifecycle. Teams initiate work by defining the problem, intended outcomes, stakeholders, success measures, and initial risks. After alignment and approval, they plan scope, milestones, dependencies, and a prioritized backlog with clear acceptance criteria and a Definition of Done. Execution happens in small increments, tracked on a project board and supported by regular check-ins, reviews, testing, and progress reporting. Releases require passing quality and security checks, deployment verification, and rollback readiness; retrospectives then capture lessons and track improvement actions.

The Project Manager coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, dependencies, and communication. The Product Manager prioritizes customer and business outcomes and measures results. Developers build and test the solution, QA validates quality and acceptance criteria, and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Teams use shared artifacts—including the project one-pager, roadmap and release plan, backlog, risk register, and retrospective actions—to maintain alignment and transparency.
